## OVERVIEW — Version 1: Customer-first (RECOMMENDED)

~1,950 chars. Second person where it counts, no founder ego, keyword-rich for
LinkedIn search ("student visa", "UKVI", "IRCC", "Pakistan").

---

Most students find out their file was weak from the refusal letter. By then the fee is gone, the intake is missed, and the fixes that would have worked needed 28 days they no longer have.

ParchiVisa exists to move that moment forward.

We check a student's file against the published student visa rules of the four destinations Pakistani students actually apply to — the UK (UKVI), Canada (IRCC), Australia (Department of Home Affairs) and the USA (Department of State) — and return a readiness score, the specific issues that would sink the application, and what to do about each one, in order.

HOW WE WORK

Rules, not opinions. Every finding comes from published official criteria: funds and the 28-day maintenance window, sponsor evidence, CAS and I-20, English requirements, ties and intent. Each one is traceable to the government source it came from, with the date we last reviewed it.

The score is computed, not generated. Our scoring is deterministic — the same file always produces the same verdict. AI helps explain findings in plain language. It is never allowed to decide whether an applicant passes, or to introduce a figure the rules didn't produce.

Free to find out where you stand. Your readiness score and the list of gaps cost nothing. The full report — findings, evidence requirements, document checklist and a fix-by-fix action plan — is PKR 1,500, one time.

FOR CONSULTANTS AND AGENCIES

ParchiVisa for Teams gives advisors a shared workspace to run readiness checks across a client roster, so weak files are caught and triaged before they're lodged.

WHAT WE ARE NOT

ParchiVisa is not an immigration agent and not a law firm. We don't file your application, we don't represent you, and we will never promise you a visa. We tell you what an assessing officer is likely to see — while you still have time to change it.

## OVERVIEW — Version 3: B2B / partner-first

Use ONLY if the near-term goal is signing consultancies rather than students.

---

ParchiVisa is a readiness layer for student visa applications.

Before a file is lodged, we assess it against the published criteria of UKVI, IRCC, the Australian Department of Home Affairs and the US Department of State, and return a scored breakdown: hard blockers, risk flags, evidence gaps, and a prioritised remediation plan.

Scoring is deterministic and source-traceable. Every finding maps to a published rule and the date that rule was last reviewed. AI is used to explain findings, never to determine them — so two advisors assessing the same file get the same verdict.

FOR EDUCATION CONSULTANTS AND AGENCIES

ParchiVisa for Teams provides a shared workspace to triage a client roster: run readiness checks, see which files are lodgeable and which aren't, and hand clients a branded report with a concrete action plan. Fewer refusals, less unbillable rework, and a defensible record of what was flagged and when.

FOR STUDENTS

Readiness score and gap list are free. The full report is PKR 1,500, one time.

ParchiVisa is not an immigration agent or a law firm. We do not file applications or represent applicants.

## BEFORE YOU PUBLISH — fix list

1. **An empty company page is worse than no company page.** A page with no
   logo, no banner, zero posts and one employee makes the product look
   abandoned. Have the logo, banner and 3 posts ready to go on day one, or
   don't publish yet.

2. **Every claim on this page must survive a click.** The overview promises a
   free score and a source-traceable finding. Both are true in the product —
   keep them true. If you paywall the score later, change this page the same day.

3. **The `last_reviewed` dates in `country_sources.py` still read 2025-01-15.**
   This overview explicitly sells "the date we last reviewed it." Publishing
   that claim while the underlying dates are over a year old is the one thing
   here a competitor or an angry customer could hold up. Update them first.

4. **Pick ONE price and keep it consistent** across the site, the personal
   profile and this page. PKR 1,500 is what the hero currently shows. If that
   changes, all three change together.

5. **Don't post the founder story on the company page.** It belongs on your
   personal profile, where it converts. The company page's job is trust, not
   biography.
